Verdant Flow

Verdant Flow reimagines Yuhu Plaza on Zhejiang University's Zijingang Campus as a connected ecological landscape, replacing fragmented open space with continuous planting, paths, and places for everyday campus life.

Individual Project: Tingxiao Wu

Landscape Design · Campus Public Space · Ecological Design

OPENING A FRAGMENTED CAMPUS EDGE

Yuhu Plaza is surrounded by large academic buildings and disconnected landscape edges. Limited drainage, visually repetitive routes, and underused paved space weakened its role as a shared campus destination.

The proposal breaks these spatial boundaries by extending greenery across the site and turning movement routes into a continuous landscape experience.

ORGANIZING MOVEMENT THROUGH GREEN NODES

Five spatial zones structure the proposal: a tree plaza, a curved passage, a circular lawn, garden rooms, and planted corridors. Major sightlines converge around the central plaza while smaller paths create slower routes through the landscape.

The circular lawn supports gathering and informal activity, while the tree plaza provides a shaded threshold for daily movement across the campus.

DESIGNING FOR SEASONAL CHANGE

Layered trees, flowering understory plants, grasses, and groundcover create changing colour, shade, and enclosure throughout the year. The planting strategy turns seasonal succession into part of the spatial experience.

Curving paths bring people close to planted areas, while open lawns and garden clearings balance ecological character with flexible public use.
