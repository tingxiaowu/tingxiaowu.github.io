ArchiTone: A LEGO-Inspired Gamified System for Visualized Music Education

Jiaxing Yu, Tieyao Zhang, Songruoyao Wu, Xinda Wu, Tingxiao Wu, Yanjun Chen, and Kejun Zhang

Overview

ArchiTone visualises music-theory concepts as musical blocks and buildings. Its Learning Mode supports recognition of common musical blocks through familiar works, while Creation Mode lets learners combine blocks to produce new musical works. The study reported improved engagement and learning outcomes compared with traditional approaches.

Project contributions

  1. 01A construction-inspired representation of abstract music-theory concepts.
  2. 02Learning and creation modes that combine tangible exploration with gamified activities.
  3. 03User studies evaluating engagement, understanding, and application of music theory.
