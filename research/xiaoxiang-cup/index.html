Dongpu’s Brew: A Legacy That Grows Richer with Age (东浦之酿，“历”“酒”弥香)

This landscape design proposal reconnects waterfront ecology and public life with the rice-wine heritage of Dongpu in Shaoxing Huangjiu Town.

Project Lead: Tingxiao Wu · Collaborators: Zhexuan Zhu and Zhihao Hu

Landscape Design · Cultural Heritage · Public Space

Third Prize · Undergraduate Division · 8th “Xiaoxiang Cup” Zhejiang Province College Student Landscape Architecture Design Competition

BACKGROUND

The Dashu River waterfront sits within a town strongly associated with Shaoxing rice wine. The site carries cultural memory and ecological value, but these qualities were not fully connected to everyday movement, recreation, or the experience of the river.

Our goal was to make the waterfront active and accessible while allowing the town's wine-making history to remain legible in the landscape.

DESIGN CONCEPTION

The proposal is organized around two connected themes: leisure and culture. A continuous river path links daily recreation with spaces that interpret the production, exchange, and communal memory of rice wine.

Instead of treating heritage as an isolated display, the design embeds it in movement, planting, waterfront views, and a sequence of public activity nodes.

LANDSCAPE STRATEGY

Shoreline paths and activity spaces improve access to the water. Wetland landscapes and seasonal planting support ecological character, while framed views and culturally informed details create moments for learning, gathering, and rest.

The resulting sequence moves between quieter ecological areas and more social nodes, allowing local residents and visitors to encounter the waterfront at different speeds.

OUTCOME

The final proposal combines a master plan, planting and ecological strategies, spatial details, and perspective visualizations. It received Third Prize in the undergraduate division of the 8th “Xiaoxiang Cup” Zhejiang Province College Student Landscape Architecture Design Competition in 2023.

As project lead, I coordinated the concept, spatial strategy, design development, and final presentation with the project team.
