Beautwilight

Beautwilight is a speculative VR platform for personalized reflection, meaningful activity, and family connection in hospice contexts.

Individual Project: Tingxiao Wu

Immersive Media · Service Design · Experience Prototyping

Watch project video ↗

BACKGROUND

Serious illness can limit a person's mobility and energy, making travel, shared activities, and visits to meaningful places more difficult. Digital experiences may expand what remains possible, but they must be designed with care around comfort, effort, consent, and emotional sensitivity.

I explored how an immersive platform might support personal wishes and family connection without treating technology as a substitute for hospice care.

RESEARCH SYNTHESIS

The exploratory research was synthesized into three design priorities: personalization, so an experience can reflect an individual's memories and wishes; family connection, so activities can be shared; and low-burden operation, so the interaction remains understandable and flexible.

These priorities shifted the concept away from one fixed virtual world and toward a connected set of experiences that can be configured around the person.

EXPERIENCE CONCEPTION

Explore allows a person to visit existing environments or create a personalized world from a text prompt. Healing offers calm environments and culturally meaningful reflective activities.

Play provides lightweight games and movement-oriented experiences. Family supports shared virtual activities and a memory archive organized around places, dates, images, and stories.

HOW I MADE IT

I developed the project as a high-fidelity interaction concept and an end-to-end usage scenario. The prototype connects prompt-based environment creation, panoramic scene generation, guided activities, shared family spaces, and a cloud-based life-story library.

The concept video shows how a person could move between these modules and how one memory could become an environment, an activity, and a record that can be revisited.

Prototype flow forthcoming

SCOPE & REFLECTION

Beautwilight is a speculative experience prototype, not a medical device or a clinically evaluated intervention. The project demonstrates a design direction; it does not provide evidence of clinical benefit.

A responsible next step would involve participatory design with patients, family members, and hospice professionals, followed by careful study of accessibility, physical comfort, consent, and the emotional consequences of recording or sharing memories.
