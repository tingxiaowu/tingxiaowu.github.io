Abstract

PneuStep examines how compact pneumatic structures beneath the foot can enrich virtual walking experiences. The wearable prototype coordinates soft actuation with immersive scenes to communicate differences between terrain conditions while retaining wearability and physical safety.

Research contributions

  1. 01A wearable soft-pneumatic architecture for underfoot feedback.
  2. 02Terrain-oriented haptic patterns coordinated with virtual walking scenes.
  3. 03A working demonstration designed for repeatable public interaction.
