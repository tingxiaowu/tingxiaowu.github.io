Breatho: Real-Time Respiratory Guidance System for Amateur Runners

Yuheng Tao, Tingxiao Wu, and Shaoyu Cai

ACM SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Emerging Technologies · Accepted

Overview

Breatho is a waist-worn pneumatic breathing guidance system that helps amateur runners coordinate breathing rhythm with running cadence. The system uses two soft lateral airbags placed around the abdomen to deliver rhythmic press-and-release cues corresponding to inhalation and exhalation. By turning respiratory guidance into a bodily haptic experience, Breatho explores an embodied alternative to visual, auditory, or vibrotactile breathing cues during movement. An IMU-based sensing module detects running cadence, and an ESP32-based pneumatic controller maps detected steps to breathing cycles through locomotor-respiratory coupling (LRC). Miniature pumps and solenoid valves then generate synchronised abdominal pressure cues. The prototype supports live demonstrations through a web interface, real-time pressure visualisation, and multiple breathing modes, including cadence-driven running guidance and timer-based rhythmic breathing. Preliminary evaluations suggest that users can understand the pneumatic cues, that dual small lateral airbags provide clearer and more comfortable feedback, and that Breatho may support more stable breathing rhythm and reduced perceived exertion. Breatho contributes a working demonstration of step-synchronised pneumatic haptics for sports training, respiratory awareness, and embodied interaction.
