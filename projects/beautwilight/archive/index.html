Beautwilight

Beautwilight is an individual design exploration of how people in hospice contexts might revisit meaningful places, share experiences with family, and preserve personal stories through VR.

Individual Project: Tingxiao Wu

Immersive Media · Service Design · Experience Prototyping

MAKING ROOM FOR PERSONAL WISHES

A wish might be to revisit a familiar city, take part in a religious tradition, or spend time with family. Beautwilight began with the question of how to make room for these personal experiences when travel and shared activities are difficult to arrange.

I explored a VR experience that connects choosing a meaningful place, sharing an activity, and preserving a memory. The design considers three participants in that journey: the person receiving care, their family, and the staff who help them use the system.

LISTENING TO HOSPICE STAFF

I interviewed staff across six hospice-care institutions about using VR to support patients' personal wishes. Their concerns were practical: limited room and budgets, equipment maintenance, unfamiliar controls, and discomfort during use. They also emphasized family relationships and culturally meaningful activities.

These conversations shaped the design priorities. A small set of entry points addresses ease of operation; configurable environments respond to different interests and traditions; shared spaces and a story archive keep family connection central. Portability, cost, and comfort remain requirements for developing the concept into equipment suitable for care settings.

FOUR WAYS TO PARTICIPATE

Explore brings together panoramic visits and a creative mode for describing a personal environment. Healing groups religious settings, meditation, nature, and music into a space for reflection.

Play offers games and movement-oriented activities. Family proposes a shared virtual home for conversation and activities, alongside a Life Story archive organized by time, place, and personal description. These modules let the user choose a kind of experience before navigating its individual controls.

FROM A TEXT PROMPT TO A VIRTUAL PLACE

For personalized environments, I explored a workflow connecting Skybox AI and Unity. The code sends a text prompt to the Skybox API, receives a generated image URL, downloads the image, and applies it as a texture to a Unity Skybox material.

This gives the creative mode a concrete technical starting point: a description can become the panoramic background of a scene. The interface prototype then shows how a user would enter that environment and move between activities. Interactive objects, shared sessions, and the cloud story archive belong to the broader service concept.

BRINGING THE EXPERIENCE TOGETHER

As an individual project, Beautwilight brings together stakeholder research, the service structure, interface design, and a scene-generation exploration. The walkthrough follows the experience from selecting preferences to exploring places and sharing personal stories.

The next step would be to test a shorter journey with patients, family members, and hospice staff: choose a place, spend time there, and leave comfortably. The current work establishes a design concept and prototype workflow; it does not establish therapeutic benefit.
