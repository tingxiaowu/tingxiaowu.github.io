Overview

This work investigates how geometric encoding in liquid-metal microchannels can increase the information carried by a small number of sensing channels. A four-channel soft tactile skin combines position-dependent resistance responses with data-driven decoding to estimate touch location and normal force.

My contribution

  1. 01Four-channel topology design and fabrication-process development.
  2. 02Sensor characterisation experiments, including localisation performance, sensitivity, and hysteresis.
  3. 03Data analysis and development of interactive application demonstrations.
