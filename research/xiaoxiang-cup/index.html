Dongpu’s Brew: A Legacy That Grows Richer with Age (东浦之酿，“历”“酒”弥香)

Tingxiao Wu, Zhexuan Zhu, and Zhihao Hu

Third Prize · Undergraduate Division · December 8, 2023

Overview

“东浦之酿，‘历’‘酒’弥香” is a landscape design proposal for revitalising the Dashu River waterfront vitality belt in Shaoxing Huangjiu Town. The proposal reconnects waterfront recreation with the area’s rice-wine heritage through two themes—leisure and culture—and a sequence of public spaces along the river. Planting, shoreline paths, wetland landscapes, and activity nodes create an accessible waterfront for residents and visitors while preserving local memory and seasonal ecological character. The project received Third Prize in the undergraduate division of the 8th “Xiaoxiang Cup” Zhejiang Province College Student Landscape Architecture Design Competition.
