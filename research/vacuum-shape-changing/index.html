Overview

This ongoing project studies low-profile vacuum-driven pouches whose internal open-guide geometry encodes angular motion. Multiple guides can be arranged within one pouch to create compound motion while sharing a single pressure line.

Research direction

  1. 01Guide geometry as a physical program for angular range and stopping behaviour.
  2. 02Multiple joints and compound motion within one low-profile pneumatic pouch.
  3. 03A fabrication and calibration workflow connecting guide geometry to motion.
