PneuStep: A Soft Pneumatic-Based Foot Haptic Device for Walking Experience on Different Terrains in VR

Huzhaorui Kang, Zhuolun Li, Tingxiao Wu, Yuheng Tao, Quanhao Gan, Tao Lin, Kongpyung (Justin) Moon, and Shaoyu Cai

ACM SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Emerging Technologies · Accepted

Overview

PneuStep is a soft pneumatic foot-haptic device designed to render terrain-related sensations to the medial foot arch during virtual walking. A pair of shoes integrates flexible silicone haptic modules, each containing five independently controlled pneumatic units fabricated from materials with different stiffness. A custom pneumatic system controls pumps and high-frequency solenoid valves to produce both sustained force and vibratory feedback, supporting virtual experiences such as grass, sand, stone, ocean waves, and foot-clutch interaction. Technical characterisation showed forces up to 6.36 N across actuation pressures from 10 to 70 kPa and controllable vibration from 10 to 100 Hz, establishing a compact platform for terrain simulation and fuller-body immersion in VR.
