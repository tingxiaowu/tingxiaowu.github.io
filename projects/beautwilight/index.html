Overview

Beautwilight explores how immersive media might support meaningful experiences for people whose mobility and energy are limited by serious illness. The concept brings personalized scene creation, guided activities, shared family spaces, and memory preservation into one VR platform. Rather than presenting a fixed virtual world, it proposes a configurable experience in which a person can revisit meaningful places, create new environments, spend time with family members, and record life stories.

Experience Architecture

The portfolio research was synthesized into three priorities: personalization, family connection, and low-burden operation. These priorities led to four connected modules rather than a single linear experience.

ExploreVisit existing environments or create a personalized world from a text prompt.

HealingEnter calm environments and culturally meaningful reflective activities.

PlayAccess lightweight games and movement-oriented experiences.

FamilyShare virtual activities and preserve stories through places, dates, images, and memories.

Prototype and Scope

The project was developed as a high-fidelity interaction concept and an end-to-end usage scenario. It explores prompt-based environment creation, panoramic scene generation, shared activities, and a cloud-based life-story library. The concept video communicates how a user could move between these experiences; it should not be presented as evidence of clinical benefit.

Beautwilight is a speculative experience prototype, not a medical device or a clinically evaluated intervention. A future iteration would require participatory design with patients, family members, and hospice professionals, with careful attention to comfort, accessibility, consent, and the emotional consequences of recording or sharing memories.
