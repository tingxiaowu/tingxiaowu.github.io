Capacitive Super-Resolution Sensing

Co-leading · Ongoing Research

Project image placeholderPublic-safe project imagery will be added later.

Overview

This ongoing project develops a three-channel capacitive sensor for super-resolution contact localisation on three-dimensional surfaces. The approach uses greedy topology search to maximise separability among the three channel responses while reducing spatial aliasing, followed by machine-learning-based localisation.
