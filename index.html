About

I build interactive materials and physical interfaces that can sense, move, and communicate.

I am an MSc student in Engineering Design & Innovation at the National University of Singapore and a Student Research Assistant in the Immersive Reality Lab.

My research sits at the intersection of human–computer interaction, soft and wearable sensing, digital fabrication, haptics, and shape-changing interfaces. My current work spans liquid-metal tactile sensing, pneumatic interaction, wearable systems, and multisensory experiences in virtual reality. I am preparing for PhD study in HCI and related areas.
