DualShade: A Fail-Safe Dual-Layer Adaptive Eyewear for Real-Time Glare and Luminance Control

Tingxiao Wu, Yuheng Tao, and Shaoyu Cai

Course Project · Research Prototype

Overview

Sudden luminance transitions and localized glare can temporarily reduce the readability of visual information. Conventional countermeasures often respond too globally or too coarsely: sunglasses darken the whole field of view, photochromic lenses may respond slowly or inconsistently, and visors can block important visual cues. We present DualShade, a camera-free dual-layer adaptive eyewear that decouples scene-wide luminance adaptation from localized glare suppression. Layer 1 provides smooth global electro-optic tinting for rapid brightness transitions, while Layer 2 uses a transparent LCD mask to selectively attenuate one of three zones for directional glare hotspots. A lightweight sensing pipeline combines global and directional illuminance sensing, and a confidence-gated control policy returns the system to a transparent state under low light or uncertainty. In the live demonstration, visitors wear DualShade and experience global dimming, localized zonal masking, combined response, and fail-safe transparency in a controlled tabletop visual scene.
