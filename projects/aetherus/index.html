Aetherus

Aetherus is a camera-equipped device concept for exploring a place through Feng Shui and local-space astrology. A rotating ring switches between the two interpretations and a combined view.

Project Lead: Tingxiao Wu

Tangible Interaction · Cultural Computing · Product Design

TWO WAYS OF READING A PLACE

People use many cultural frameworks to interpret the relationship between themselves and a place. Feng Shui and local-space astrology come from different histories, symbols, and assumptions, yet both connect spatial direction with personal meaning.

The project explores how one object could make these differences visible and invite comparison rather than collapsing them into a single answer.

Product render forthcoming

ONE OBJECT, THREE PERSPECTIVES

The proposed flow begins with personal context and an image of a room or site. The interface then presents a Feng Shui interpretation, an astrology interpretation, or a combined view. Keeping the modes visible makes the selected perspective an explicit part of the interaction.

When it is not being used for spatial exploration, the object becomes an ambient display inspired by traditional talisman forms.

Three interface modes forthcoming

TURN, COMPARE, AND PREVIEW

The adjustment ring is the main mode selector. In the interaction concept, turning it changes the interpretation and visual language on the screen. Users can inspect a recommendation and activate an Adjust control to preview a proposed spatial change.

The design pairs a physical gesture for changing perspective with screen controls for inspecting its consequences. This lets the same captured place anchor the comparison, rather than requiring the user to restart the task in each mode.

SEPARATING THE CONCEPT FROM THE PROTOTYPE

The proof of concept connects an ESP32-CAM to a cloud-based multimodal model. A captured image is encoded and sent for text-based interpretation, while a separate interface prototype demonstrates mode switching and the presentation of recommendations.

The product rendering communicates the intended form; the interface prototype communicates the user flow; the camera-to-cloud experiment explores technical feasibility. These are distinct pieces of evidence, not a claim that every proposed function is integrated into a finished device.

System flow forthcoming

INTERPRETATION, NOT MEASUREMENT

Aetherus treats Feng Shui and astrology as cultural and interpretive belief systems. The device does not scientifically measure luck, energy, or environmental quality, and its outputs should not be framed as objective spatial analysis.

A future iteration could make the sources and uncertainty behind each response more visible, and focus on supporting reflection and conversation instead of producing an authoritative recommendation.
