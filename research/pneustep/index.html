PneuStep: A Soft Pneumatic-Based Foot Haptic Device for Walking Experience on Different Terrains in VR

Huzhaorui Kang, Zhuolun Li, Tingxiao Wu, Yuheng Tao, Quanhao Gan, Tao Lin, Kongpyung (Justin) Moon, and Shaoyu Cai

StatusAccepted
VenueSIGGRAPH Asia 2026 E-Tech
AreaFoot haptics · VR

Overview

PneuStep is a wearable soft-pneumatic foot-haptic device designed to enrich virtual walking experiences by rendering terrain-related sensations beneath the foot.

Project contributions

  1. 01A wearable soft-pneumatic architecture for underfoot feedback.
  2. 02Terrain-oriented haptic patterns coordinated with virtual walking scenes.
  3. 03An integrated demonstration designed for repeatable public interaction.
