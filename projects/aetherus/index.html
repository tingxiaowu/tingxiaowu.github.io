Aetherus

Aetherus is a camera-equipped device concept for exploring a place through Feng Shui and local-space astrology. A rotating ring switches between the two interpretations and a combined view.

Project Lead: Tingxiao Wu

Tangible Interaction · Cultural Computing · Product Design

TWO WAYS OF READING A PLACE

The project began with two ways of interpreting a place: Feng Shui and local-space astrology. Our research compared their concepts, tools, and spatial applications, and included interviews with a Taoist practitioner and a practitioner of environmental psychology and astrology.

The design problem was that the two frameworks can offer different advice about the same setting, while their specialist language is difficult for a newcomer to navigate. We explored a portable object that lets the user choose a perspective and inspect its suggestions in context.

MAKING THE CHANGE OF PERSPECTIVE TANGIBLE

The rotating ring is the main mode selector, switching between Feng Shui, astrology, and a combined mode. Illuminated symbols indicate which interpretation is active, making the selected framework visible on the object itself.

The circular gesture draws on the ritual and cyclical imagery explored in the research. Soft curves and a rounded pentagonal body extend that symbolic language into the form. When idle, the screen displays a pattern inspired by traditional Chinese talismans, allowing the object to remain part of the room.

FROM A SUGGESTION TO A SPATIAL PREVIEW

The interface journey starts with personal information and a view of a room or site. Suggestions appear over that view; red outlines locate the areas they refer to, while smaller details receive an additional visual marker.

An Adjust switch replaces the suggestion banner with a proposed visualization of the change. Switching it off returns to the original view. The interaction concept lets the user move between an explanation and its spatial consequence, then change modes to explore another interpretation.

PROTOTYPING CAPTURE AND INTERPRETATION

The technical exploration uses an ESP32-CAM with an OV2640 camera. The captured image is encoded as Base64 and sent to a Tongyi Qwen VL model for image analysis; a subsequent chatbot request produces a Feng Shui interpretation.

A separate desktop sketch uses pointer angle and click events to simulate switching between interface images. Together, the camera-to-cloud code and interaction sketch explore two parts of the concept: generating a response from a captured scene and navigating the display. The physical ring and live adjustment preview are represented in the product and interface designs.

A CULTURAL INTERFACE FOR EVERYDAY SPACES

The outcome brings together product form, interface sequences, and technical explorations for looking at interiors and building sites through different cultural frameworks. Its central interaction is the movement between perspectives: turn the ring, inspect a suggestion, and preview an alternative.

Feng Shui and astrology are treated here as interpretive belief systems, not scientific measurements of luck or energy. A next iteration would make the source of each suggestion clearer and explore how people understand disagreement between the two modes.
